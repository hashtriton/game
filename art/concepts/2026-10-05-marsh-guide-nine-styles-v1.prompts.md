```text

Create ONE high-resolution SQUARE comparison contact sheet, a precise 3 by 3 grid of NINE equally sized panels. The same original game character appears ONCE in every panel, in nine clearly and substantially different art/rendering styles. This is a style selection board for a future 3D fantasy game. Each panel must be large, detailed and legible.

Input image role: identity and equipment reference ONLY. Select ONLY the adult female human MARSH GUIDE standing at the FRONT LEFT of the supplied group screenshot, with dark brown hair, an olive cloak, a curved sword and a glowing turquoise lantern. Do not reproduce the other five characters, the group scene or its composition.

IDENTITY LOCK across all nine panels: the SAME adult woman, around 30, lean athletic realistic human proportions, weathered pale-to-light-olive skin, angular expressive face, short tousled dark brown hair, serious calm expression. SAME dark olive waxed-fabric cloak over brown leather armor and charcoal linen, belts, practical leather boots, curved single-edged sword in her right hand angled down toward screen left, oxidized bronze lantern at her left hip glowing turquoise. SAME full-body three-quarter ready pose, same relative camera angle gently looking down, same size and same muted olive/brown/turquoise character colors. Do not redesign costume, ethnicity, haircut or weapons between panels. Natural adult anatomy in every style, normal-sized head and eyes. Clearly visible face, both hands, sword, lantern and both boots.
Layout: exact regular 3x3 grid. EACH panel contains the full character from head to foot, with empty space around the silhouette and no clipping. Same simple muted grey-stone ground patch under every figure and quiet pale grey backdrop. Thin neutral gutters between panels. Character occupies most of its panel. NO detailed arena environment.
Numbering: label panels with ONLY large clear Arabic numerals "1" to "9", once each, at the TOP LEFT corner inside each panel, in a clean black sans-serif font on a small light area. Reading order left to right, top to bottom: first row 1 2 3, second row 4 5 6, third row 7 8 9. No words or other text anywhere.

NINE SPECIFIC STYLES - change the actual rendering language, shapes/texture treatment and shading distinctly:
1. CINEMATIC REALISM: premium realistic modern game rendering, nuanced natural skin, carefully observed leather grain and linen weave, believable physically based metal, soft natural lighting and subtle fine detail. A convincingly realistic CG character.
2. REALISTIC OIL PAINTING: the same character rendered through expressive tangible oil brush strokes, thick pigment edges, broad confident painterly planes and deep rich modeled shadows. Mature figurative painting, real anatomy, visibly painted texture rather than photographic detail.
3. HAND-PAINTED 3D: medium-complexity game geometry with confidently painted diffuse textures, broad deliberately brushed highlights and shadows, simple clean material separation, restrained stylization and beautiful game readability. Adult proportions, no huge shoulders or toy-like forms.
4. SERIOUS GRAPHIC NOVEL: sophisticated dramatic graphic shading with firm two- or three-step shadows, thin selectively placed dark ink edges, flat rich color areas and striking angular facial planes. Mature grounded anatomy, no big-eyed animation look, no chibi.
5. COLORED ENGRAVING: fine elegant directional cross-hatching and etched lines across clothing and face, shadows built from dense ink strokes, restrained olive and umber color washes with a bright turquoise lantern accent. Clearly an engraving-based visual treatment.
6. FACETED REALISM: a beautiful low-poly 3D interpretation with obviously visible large planar polygon facets throughout skin, hair and cloth, no smooth gradients on the facets, matte mineral-like color blocks. Realistic silhouette and joint proportions, sculptural and serious, not a cute toy.
7. EARLY-2000s DARK FANTASY GAME: visibly simpler angular polygon geometry, modest-resolution hand-authored cloth and skin textures, old-school game shading, atmospheric grounded aesthetic. Clear coarse texture detail, limited smoothness; still the SAME adult character and pose.
8. VOLUMETRIC WATERCOLOR: layered translucent olive, umber and grey pigment washes, soft bleeding edges and paper grain, controlled ink definition at key facial and equipment details, subtle modeled volume. Delicate atmospheric mature realism, clear full-body silhouette.
9. PIXEL-RENDERED 3D: the same realistically proportioned character represented through an intentionally chunky, sharply pixelated 3D game raster with discernible square pixels, selective dithering and controlled limited-color shading. Dark adult fantasy feel. No smooth brushwork and no cute pixel mascot.

CRITICAL: nine panels must immediately read as NINE DIFFERENT STYLES while all unmistakably show the SAME character, SAME costume, SAME pose and SAME framing. Do not make nine slightly different realistic renders. Do not let styles drift into nine different outfit designs. Every style is serious and visually beautiful, not overtly cartoonish. One character per panel, nine total depictions. No franchise designs, logos, watermark, extra people, pets, cropped body parts or extra text.
```

## Усиление различий стилей

```text
Edit this NINE-PANEL character style comparison sheet. Keep the exact 3x3 grid, all numerals 1 through 9, the SAME adult woman's identity, SAME dark hair, SAME olive cloak, brown leather armor, sword, turquoise lantern, SAME full-body pose, SAME camera, framing and scale. Improve one thing: make the actual CHARACTERS in the nine panels much more clearly different in rendering language. The purpose is selecting a whole game's visual style. Do not merely apply different backgrounds behind nearly identical realistic figures.

Leave panels 5 (engraving), 6 (faceted polygonal 3D) and 9 (pixel rendering) essentially unchanged; they already work well.
Panel 1 should remain the strongest smooth fine-detail realistic CG baseline.
Panel 2: strongly re-render the WOMAN HERSELF as a bold realistic OIL PAINTING: unmistakable thick, visible palette-knife and brush marks on her face, hair, hands, every fold and boot, painterly fragmented edges, broad luminous strokes and rich earthy shadow masses. Great figurative oil painting with an adult natural face, not a realistic CG figure placed on a painted backdrop. Reduce tiny photographic texture detail substantially.
Panel 3: make this unmistakeably HAND-PAINTED GAME 3D with simplified clean shapes and broad painted color transitions on skin and clothing, chunky deliberately painted highlights and shaded folds, almost no photographic grain; adult semi-real proportions. Make the face and cloth read as painted surfaces, with modest geometry and softer shape transitions. This is a polished restrained hand-painted fantasy game model, not a hyperreal model.
Panel 4: seriously strengthen GRAPHIC NOVEL rendering on the character: clean visible dark ink outlines, bold two-tone light/shadow divisions over skin and folds, sharply designed dark shadow shapes and flatter large olive, brown and cream color regions. Keep realistic adult anatomy and dignified face. A sophisticated mature ink-and-color graphic style, not children's cartoons.
Panel 7: create unmistakable early-2000s low-detail RETRO 3D game rendering: much simpler polygon geometry, angular hands and hair chunks, low-resolution pixelated skin and leather texture maps, visibly coarse texture edges, simple shading, lower-fidelity game model. Keep adult proportions, same clothes and pose. This is a true older real-time game look distinct from smooth realism and from panel 6's untextured facets; not just an ordinary realistic model with a darker cloak.
Panel 8: re-render the WOMAN HERSELF entirely as WATERCOLOUR: translucent washes and visible pigment blooms on her cloak and face, softened broken edges, a little paper showing through shadows, minimal fine leather/skin detail, delicate expressive ink accents preserving readable silhouette. Clearly watercolor character with volume, not a realistic CG figure pasted onto watercolor scenery.

Every panel is serious and aesthetically appealing. Do not introduce oversized heads, big eyes, anime faces, toy proportions, new costume designs or new characters. Maintain full body visibility and numbering. Keep quiet simple backgrounds; the art-style change must be strongest ON THE CHARACTER.
```
