# Проводница - девять глобальных художественных направлений

Дата: 5 октября 2026 года. Сгенерировано встроенным imagegen. Новый сравнительный концепт по поправке пользователя: меняются пропорции, лицо, силуэт, конструкция одежды, снаряжение и поза. Общие признаки - взрослая проводница, тёмные волосы, зелёный плащ, меч и бирюзовый фонарь. Предыдущая модель не использовалась как входное изображение, чтобы не закреплять её геометрию.

[Лист 3x3](2026-10-05-marsh-guide-global-styles-v1.png).

Игры ниже - ориентиры художественного языка, а не утверждение о точном воспроизведении их ассетов. Персонажи оригинальные. 5 октября 2026 года пользователь выбрал направление **№2, Warcraft**, с созданием собственных новых, современных и стильных героев, объектов и существ. Конкретная модель на листе не утверждена как игровой ассет; соответствующие модели и шейдеры Unity пока не реализованы. Актуальное решение - в [docs/art-direction.md](../../docs/art-direction.md).

1. Dota 2 - угловатое героическое фэнтези.
2. Warcraft - массивное героическое фэнтези.
3. Overwatch - чистые формы и спортивная стилизация.
4. Diablo IV - суровое реалистичное фэнтези.
5. Dishonored - вытянутые пропорции и архитектурные формы.
6. Final Fantasy XVI - изящное драматическое фэнтези.
7. Hades - мифологическая графичность.
8. Darkest Dungeon II - театральный мрачный гротеск.
9. No Rest for the Wicked - органически искажённые пропорции.

## Финальный промпт

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE large high-resolution SQUARE 3x3 ART-DIRECTION COMPARISON BOARD showing NINE RADICALLY DIFFERENT character DESIGNS for an original fantasy game. Every panel is a separately sculpted interpretation of the same simple character IDEA, as if nine completely different game art teams each designed her from scratch.

CRITICAL PURPOSE: Compare GLOBAL GAME ART STYLES, not rendering filters. Actually change the character's BODY PROPORTIONS, FACE DESIGN, head-to-body ratio, shoulder width, hands, boots, silhouette, costume construction, equipment geometry, pose, texture detail, material language and color blocking from panel to panel. If the nine figures have the same anatomy and clothing meshes with only different surface effects, the image has failed. Each design must look different even as a small black silhouette.
Only shared motifs: an adult female wilderness guide with dark hair, an OLIVE/GREEN CAPE OR CLOAK, a SWORD, and a TURQUOISE GLOWING LANTERN. THESE FOUR MOTIFS ARE THE ONLY IDENTITY LOCK. Her face, body, exact costume, sword and lantern shapes, pose, skin tone and secondary palette MAY AND SHOULD differ to fit each art direction. Do not reuse the same photorealistic face or same cloak silhouette. No existing franchise character costumes, logos or weapons.

Layout: exactly 3 rows by 3 columns, nine clean evenly sized panels. One LARGE COMPLETE full-body figure in each panel, head, sword, lantern and feet entirely inside its panel. Similar neutral three-quarter full-body presentation camera and roughly comparable occupied panel area, but DISTINCT expressive poses. Quiet neutral grey floor and backdrop, minimal environment. Number labels ONLY: large clear black "1" through "9" in top-left corners on light areas, row-major reading order. No names, captions or other writing. Thin panel gutters. High-detail beautiful professional game-character presentation; excellent separation.

NINE GLOBAL ART DIRECTIONS:
PANEL 1 - DOTA 2-style original fantasy hero design:
A mature angular hand-painted 3D female adventurer with a LONG TAPERED TORSO, narrow waist, slightly large hands, deliberately chiseled face and strong swept-back dark hair clumps. Huge TRIANGULAR asymmetric olive cape, one sharp sculptural shoulder guard, angular graphic color blocking, broad painted material planes. Large sickle-curved fantasy sword with a distinct hooked profile, angular faceted turquoise lantern. Low wide-legged combat stance. Russet, olive and cream contrast. A redesigned top-down fantasy hero model, not realistic medieval clothing.

PANEL 2 - WARCRAFT-style original heroic fantasy design:
A strongly built compact warrior woman with BROAD SHOULDERS, visibly larger head, very thick forearms, OVERSIZED FISTS AND BOOTS, short powerful neck, expressive sculptural face, heavy shaggy dark hair. Big layered ROUND SHOULDER ARMOR, thick chest armor and a short GREEN SPLIT CAPE. A MASSIVE chunky straight cleaver-like sword, a heavy relic lantern with thick bronze bands. Squat grounded defiant stance. Rich saturated green, warm gold, burgundy. Highly exaggerated heroic sculpted proportions and generous rounded forms, beautiful premium modern 3D finish. Absolutely not the slim anatomy from panel 1.

PANEL 3 - OVERWATCH-style original polished heroic design:
An appealing SPORTY ATHLETIC woman with long legs, slightly large expressive face, smooth stylized anatomy, strong clean CURVED shapes, asymmetrical short dark hair. A fitted contemporary-shaped fantasy explorer outfit in clean green, cream and charcoal with rounded modular armor, a SHORT STRUCTURED green cape, large beautifully curved boots and gloves. Wide smooth clean-edged sword and a compact modular turquoise energy-lantern. Energetic lively diagonal ready pose. Very clean smooth high-quality materials, polished broad highlight control. Fantasy reinterpretation of sleek hero-shooter shape language, no guns, no copied hero gear.

PANEL 4 - DIABLO IV-style original grounded grim fantasy:
A physically believable STOCKY ADULT survivor with substantial natural muscle, a scarred tired face, messy dark hair, natural small head relative to body. Thick overlapping worn rough leather, fur edging, heavy linen, muted moss cape tied in practical irregular folds. A modest practical steel sword and an iron-caged lantern with cold ghost light. Close guarded pose, bent knees, hunched weighted shoulders. Dense realistic age, dirt, seams and material wear, solemn intimidating beauty. Muddy iron, dark brown and muted grey-green. Anatomically grounded rather than clean idealized hero design.

PANEL 5 - DISHONORED-style original architectural character design:
A VERY TALL THIN narrow-shouldered woman with UNUSUALLY LONG ARMS AND FINGERS, elongated angular adult face, sharp prominent cheekbones, swept-back dark hair and slightly exaggerated nose. Long severe geometric olive coat-cape with high triangular collar, thin belted torso, angular cuffs and long narrow boots. A very long needle-like curved sword and a long narrow architectural brass lantern. Sly tilted forward pose with crooked elbow and offset hips. Sculpted painterly planes, cool muted elegant decayed materials. Strongly elongated silhouette and eccentric adult morphology, not standard model anatomy.

PANEL 6 - FINAL FANTASY XVI-style original dramatic fantasy design:
A VERY SLENDER TALL poised adult swordswoman with exceptionally LONG elegant legs, idealized delicate face, long flowing dark hair. Fitted detailed dark leather and silver armor with elaborate asymmetrical tailoring, a LONG FLOWING deep emerald mantle split into graceful ribbons, jewelry-like equipment fixtures. A long elegantly tapered bright silver blade and a filigree crystalline lantern. Graceful upright contrapposto, one foot forward. Rich realistically rendered fantasy materials, fine craftsmanship, cinematic dramatic beauty. Narrow, flowing and graceful, no thick heavy proportions.

PANEL 7 - HADES-style original mythic graphic design:
A lean adult woman with dry sharply defined SCULPTURAL MUSCULATURE, sharp expressive face, short curly black hair, exposed strong arms and one shoulder. Green cloak interpreted as BOLD ASYMMETRIC DRAPED CLOTH, simplified mythic bronze torso fittings, wraps and sandals. Large leaf-shaped sword and geometric ring-framed lantern. Dramatic broad TWIST OF THE TORSO, powerful asymmetric gesture. Gorgeous designed flat color blocks, strong angular graphic shadows, dramatic selective contours, Mediterranean mythic styling. Original costume and identity, no copied god or game hero.

PANEL 8 - DARKEST DUNGEON II-style original dark theatrical design:
A stylized heavy upper-body figure with a SMALL stern adult head, broad compressed shoulders, HUGE LONG ARMS AND HANDS and comparatively THIN SHORT LEGS. Dark green shoulder cape and an exceptionally HIGH STIFF COLLAR enclosing the jaw, compressed layers of battered cloth and angular leather. Heavy short sword, a LARGE lantern held FAR FORWARD in a huge extended gloved hand. Hunched theatrical silhouette with head low between shoulders. Dense designed black shadow masses and bold ink-edged painted 3D shapes. Slightly grotesque but compelling mature character sculpt, not a cute cartoon. Strong shape compression unlike panel 5's elongation.

PANEL 9 - NO REST FOR THE WICKED-style original organically distorted fantasy:
A sturdy pear-shaped female traveler with VERY SMALL HEAD, narrow sloping shoulders, wide hips, HEAVY LONG LOWER LEGS and broad feet, unusually asymmetric natural-seeming proportions. Short messy dark hair, long sober narrow face. Soft organically lumpy olive cape and uneven loosely folded work garments wrapped around broad hips, imperfect seams, asymmetric gear. A thick rough handcrafted sword and a softly rounded rustic turquoise lantern. Low relaxed center of gravity with one bent heavy leg. Tactile sculpted painterly surfaces, warmly lit hand-crafted material masses and an intentionally warped organic silhouette. Completely unlike slim, athletic or top-heavy bodies elsewhere.

Final requirements: NINE DIFFERENT NEW GEOMETRIC CHARACTER INTERPRETATIONS, no cosmetic filter board and no repeated identical models. Shared guide motifs make them comparable while every style's overall design grammar is immediately visible. Every figure attractive and well designed within its own art direction, adult serious fantasy, no chibi babies or giant anime eyes. All nine full figures entirely visible. No extra characters or background NPCs. No franchise logos, text except numbers, watermark or copied recognizable existing characters.
```
